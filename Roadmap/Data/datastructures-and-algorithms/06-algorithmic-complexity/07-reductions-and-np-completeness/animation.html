The Claim & the Proof

Karp reduction. A 3-CNF formula with m clauses is satisfiable if and only if its constructed graph has an independent set of size k = m. Each clause becomes a triangle (so at most one of its 3 literals can be picked); conflict edges xᵢ — ¬xᵢ forbid picking a literal and its negation together. A size-m independent set = one true literal per clause with no contradictions = a satisfying assignment.
