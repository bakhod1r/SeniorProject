The Guarantee, Measured

Algorithm. Greedily build a maximal matching M (edges sharing no vertex). Put both endpoints of every matched edge into the cover C, so |C| = 2|M|. Why it covers: M is maximal, so every edge touches a matched vertex. Why it's good: any cover must use ≥1 endpoint of each matched edge, and those edges are disjoint, so OPT ≥ |M|. Hence |C| = 2|M| ≤ 2·OPT.
