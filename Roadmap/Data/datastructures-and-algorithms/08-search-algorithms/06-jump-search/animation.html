Jump Search requires SORTED input. Phase 1 jumps by m = ⌊√n⌋, comparing only the right-edge of each block. Phase 2 linear-scans inside the located block once we overshoot the target. Total comparisons ≤ 2√n. Optimal under forward-only or asymmetric-access storage models.
