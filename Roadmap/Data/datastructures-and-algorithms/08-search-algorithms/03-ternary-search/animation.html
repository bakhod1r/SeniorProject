Step Explanation

Press Start to begin the animation, or Step to advance one iteration at a time. Each iteration computes m1 = lo + (hi-lo)/3 and m2 = hi - (hi-lo)/3, then eliminates the outer third on the losing side.

Note. Ternary search beats binary search for unimodal optimization, where "greater/less" no longer reveals the direction of the peak. On a sorted array, however, binary search wins: ternary makes 2 comparisons per step and shrinks by 2/3, giving worst-case ~2·log3/2(n) ≈ 3.42·log2(n) comparisons vs binary's log2(n). Use ternary for peaks, binary for lookups.
