Interpolation Search draws an imaginary line from (lo, arr[lo]) to (hi, arr[hi]), reads off where target lies on that line, and probes there. On UNIFORM data it converges in O(log log n) — about 5 probes for a billion elements. On SKEWED data (powers of 2) the prediction is wrong and it can degrade to O(n). Always pair with a binary fallback in production.
