A Thread Waiting on a Held Lock

The mapping. A thread wants a lock held by another thread for an unknown number of cycles W. Spin = rent: burn $1 of CPU per cycle while polling. Block = buy: pay a fixed context-switch cost of B cycles, then sleep until woken. Spin up to B cycles, then block → this is exactly ski rental, so it is 2−1/B-competitive against an oracle that knows W.

Why real futexes do this

Linux adaptive mutexes, glibc pthread spin-then-park, and the JVM's biased/adaptive locking all spin briefly before parking the thread. The spin budget is bounded by the cost of a context switch — spending up to B cycles polling before paying the B-cycle park keeps the worst-case overhead under 2× the unavoidable wait, with no knowledge of how long the lock is held.
