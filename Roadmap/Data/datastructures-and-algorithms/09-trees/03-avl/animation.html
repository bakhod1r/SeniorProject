Insert keys to grow the tree. Rotations restore the AVL invariant.

After every insert/delete, we walk back up and rebalance the first unbalanced ancestor via single (LL or RR) or double (LR or RL) rotation. Each rotation runs in O(1) and decreases the subtree height by 1.
