Square Root Decomposition

Partition the array into ⌈√n⌉ buckets. Range queries scan two partial buckets + read aggregates of every full middle bucket → O(√n) per query.

Query: O(√n) Update: O(1) for sum Space: O(n + n/B)

Tip: the partial scan touches at most B elements at each end; the full middle visits at most n/B buckets via their precomputed sum (O(1) each). Total work ≈ 2B + n/B → minimized at B = √n with value 2√n.
