Quadtree partitions 2D space into 4 quadrants (NE, NW, SE, SW) recursively. When a leaf's bucket overflows its capacity, it subdivides into 4 children. Range queries prune any subtree whose bbox does not intersect the query rectangle — that pruning is the source of the speedup over linear scans. Try clicking many points in one region to see clustered subdivision.
