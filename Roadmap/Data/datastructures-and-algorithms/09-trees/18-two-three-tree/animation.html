2-3 Tree

A perfectly height-balanced search tree where every internal node is a 2-node (1 key, 2 children) or a 3-node (2 keys, 3 children), and every leaf sits at the same depth. It is exactly a B-tree of order 3 (minimum degree t = 2).

Search: O(log n) Insert: O(log n) Delete: O(log n) All leaves at equal depth

Ready. Insert keys to grow the tree, or run a demo. In a 2-3 tree a node never holds more than two keys: a third key overflows, forcing a split whose middle key is pushed up to the parent.

Insert descends to a leaf and splits any overflowing node bottom-up, growing a new root when the split reaches the top. Delete swaps an internal key with its in-order predecessor/successor so removal happens at a leaf, then repairs underflow by borrowing from a 3-node sibling (rotation through the parent) or merging with a sibling (pulling the separator key down), cascading upward and possibly collapsing the root.
