The Idea

Reduced cost: c[i][j] - u[i] - v[j]
A tight edge has reduced cost 0.

Match on tight edges. When stuck, raise reached rows' u and lower reached columns' v by the slack δ — exposing a new tight edge without breaking reduced ≥ 0.

Optimality Certificate

When a perfect tight matching exists:
total cost = Σu + Σv
— the dual value proves the optimum.
