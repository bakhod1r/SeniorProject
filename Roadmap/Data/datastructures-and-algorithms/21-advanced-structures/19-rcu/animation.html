RCU — Read-Copy-Update

Readers traverse the version they subscribed to with no lock. A writer copies the data, modifies the copy, and atomically swaps the pointer (publish). The old version is reclaimed only after a grace period — once every pre-existing reader has finished. No reader ever sees freed memory.

Event log

RCU = near-zero read cost. The price: writers copy (copy-on-write), publish one pointer (release ordering), and defer freeing the old version until a grace period passes (no use-after-free). Used heavily in the Linux kernel for routing tables, the dcache, and read-mostly config.
