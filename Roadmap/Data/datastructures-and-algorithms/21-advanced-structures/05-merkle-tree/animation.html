Merkle Tree (Hash Tree)

Leaves are hashes of data blocks; each parent hashes its two children. The single root commits to everything. Watch a leaf change ripple up, and an inclusion proof (authentication path) fold up to the root.

Toy hash (FNV-1a, 4 hex digits) is used for readability — real Merkle trees use SHA-256 with domain separation (0x00 leaves, 0x01 nodes). Structure and propagation are identical. Variants: Merkle–Patricia trie (Ethereum), sparse Merkle tree, Merkle Mountain Range, Merkle DAG (Git/IPFS).
