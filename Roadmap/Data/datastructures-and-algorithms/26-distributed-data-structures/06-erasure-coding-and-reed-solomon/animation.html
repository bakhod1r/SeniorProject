Erasure Coding & Reed–Solomon — Any k of n Survive

A file is split into k data symbols; Reed–Solomon adds m = n−k parity symbols computed over a finite field. Lose any up to m of the n disks and the original is recovered exactly by solving a small linear system. Click disks to fail them.
